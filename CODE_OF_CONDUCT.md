# Code of conduct

Subway Seat follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, give and take feedback gracefully, and keep criticism about the work. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks aren't welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

If someone's behavior makes the project worse for you, tell the maintainer, [@oddurs](https://github.com/oddurs), privately through the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/oddurs/subway-seat/security/advisories/new), which only the maintainer can read. It's meant for security issues, but conduct reports are welcome there too; say it's about conduct in the title. For content that breaks GitHub's own rules, you can also [report it to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove comments, lock threads or block people who don't follow these guidelines.
