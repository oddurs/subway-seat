# Code of conduct

Subway Seat follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, give and take feedback gracefully, and keep criticism about the work. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks aren't welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

If someone's behavior makes the project worse for you, contact the maintainer privately at the email on [@oddurs](https://github.com/oddurs)'s GitHub profile, or open a private report through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove comments, lock threads or block people who don't follow these guidelines.
